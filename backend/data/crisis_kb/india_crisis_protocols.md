# India crisis support protocols (vetted checklist)

This knowledge base is used to ground the AEGIS crisis chatbot responses.
It should only contain vetted, non-speculative guidance and public emergency/helpline information.

## Immediate safety (first 30–60 seconds)

- Ask if the person is physically safe *right now* and where they are.
- If there is immediate physical danger or they are being followed / threatened in person:
  - Call **Police: 100** immediately.
  - If possible, move to a safer, public place and stay with other people.
- Encourage contacting a trusted person nearby (family, friend, neighbor) to stay with them.

## What to do with online harassment / threats

- **Do not engage** with the abuser. Avoid escalating replies.
- **Block and report** the account/number on the platform (WhatsApp/Instagram/etc.).
- **Preserve evidence**:
  - Take screenshots (include phone number, username, timestamps).
  - Save chat exports if available.
  - Keep threatening voicemails, emails, and any links.

## When to escalate

Escalate urgently if there is:
- A direct threat of violence, stalking, doxxing, blackmail/extortion, or sexual violence
- The person knows the victim’s location / workplace / family details
- Repeated harassment across multiple channels

## Public helplines (India)

- **Police emergency:** 100
- **Women Helpline:** 1091
- **NCW (National Commission for Women):** 7827170170
- **Cyber Crime Helpline:** 1930

## Supportive language guidelines (tone + boundaries)

- Validate: “This is not your fault. You’re not overreacting.”
- Keep guidance short and step-by-step (2–4 sentences per message).
- Ask one question at a time.
- Do not promise outcomes. Do not threaten retaliation.

## Self-harm or suicide risk (safety escalation)

If the user mentions self-harm / suicide or they may harm someone else:
- Encourage immediate help from local emergency services and a trusted person nearby.
- Keep the response short, calm, and focused on immediate safety.
